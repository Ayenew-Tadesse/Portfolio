Hid-Go Flight Booking App

Redesigning the booking flow to help travelers search, compare, and book flights with less friction on mobile.

RoleProduct Designer
PlatformMobile app (iOS/Android)
ToolsFigma, FigJam
FocusResearch, flows, UI

Overview

Hid-Go needed a mobile booking experience that could stand up to established travel apps. The existing flow asked too much of users too early, and the search-to-booking path had too many steps for what should be a quick task on a small screen.

The problem

Travelers were dropping off midway through booking. Flight search results were dense and hard to scan, filters were buried, and the checkout flow mixed low-priority details in with the steps that actually mattered — picking a flight and paying. On mobile, this meant a lot of scrolling, backtracking, and abandoned sessions.

Process

I mapped the existing booking journey end-to-end to find where users were dropping off, then rebuilt the flow around the three moments that actually drive a booking decision: search, compare, and confirm. Wireframes went through several rounds in Figma before moving to high-fidelity screens, with a lightweight design system (spacing, type scale, and reusable components) so the flow stayed consistent from search results through payment confirmation.

Solution

The redesigned flow simplifies flight search into a cleaner, scannable results list, surfaces filters that travelers actually use, and streamlines checkout into a shorter, clearer sequence. The result is a booking experience that feels closer to the speed and clarity of established travel apps, tailored to Hid-Go's own users.

Outcome
